## All for His Glory! - Part 7

Sep 27, 2026 • Pastor Wayne Frye

Drawing from Matthew 5:13-16, we are reminded that we are the salt of the earth and the light of the world, placed here with a purpose that extends far beyond ourselves. Like a lighthouse standing firm on the coastline, our lives are meant to guide others away from danger and toward the Father. The question we must each sit with is a searching one: is our light bright enough and clear enough that those around us can trace it back to God?

This message challenges us to move beyond a private, convenient faith and step into an authentic, visible relationship with God that cannot be mistaken for anything artificial. In a world increasingly shaped by imitation, the real thing stands out, and we are called to be exactly that. It's all for His glory!
