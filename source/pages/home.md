## WELCOME HOME

Faith Christian Center International is a place where people can meet Jesus, engage in life-giving community, and everyone is welcome. We believe WITH JESUS you are destined to win! We believe in creating a space where people can have authentic encounters with Christ, discover their gifts and use them for God's glory, and it's the driving force behind everything we do.

Join us for one of our services!

What We Believe

# REACH

## PEOPLE FROM ALL WALKS OF LIFE

Our prayer for every encounter is that you would feel welcomed and loved and that you would find Faith Christian Center a place where you can belong. Faith Christian Center is a contemporary, culturally relevant, and growing church that is reaching people from all walks of life and helping them experience the new life Jesus came to bring. We want you to leave every encounter amazed at God’s radical love expressed through the finished work of Jesus Christ. If you don’t remember anything except how radically awesome Jesus is, we view that as a huge success!

# TEACH

## THE TRUTH OF GOD'S WORD

Our commitment to you is to teach the truth of the word of God in a relevant and practical way so that you can apply it to everyday life. Jesus said that the truth will make you free. The teaching team of Faith Christian Center is committed to help you understand what you hear so that freedom can be experienced. You will leave every encounter equipped with the knowledge to live in victory!

# Transform

## LIVES WITH THE LOVE AND POWER OF GOD.

Our desire at Faith Christian Center is that you not only experience the love of God but also the power of God because one encounter with God can change everything! This desire is what fuels our passion for creating an atmosphere for a life changing worship experience. We don't want you to leave the same, in Jesus Name! Whether you are hurting, seeking or just need answers for life, we believe you will receive every need met as you are being transformed by the love and power of God.
