God did not create us to do life alone. On the contrary, He perfectly and intentionally designed the Church as a body of believers to benefit from the wisdom and encouragement of others that cause us to reflect on Jesus. We need each other. Through mutual support and guidance, we can strive to become our best selves. The Women of POWER ministry has established S.H.E., a community rooted in scripture, where our lives can thrive and be transformed by its power.

S.H.E. is a ministry at Faith Christian Center International that empowers women to become the best version of themselves in every area of life: 

spirit, soul, and body.

S.H.E. is an acronym for See Her Evolve, a comprehensive mentorship program designed to guide participants through their spiritual journey in Christ Jesus, from the elementary to the more advanced. By participating in S.H.E., the mentees gain a more profound understanding and purpose in God through in-person sessions, impartations, mentorship from Dr. Wendi, divine revelations from the Word of God, encouragement from small group leaders, and support from a community of sisters. Through S.H.E., mentees gain a deeper understanding and purpose in God. The program leads to self-reflection and mind renewal, ultimately producing transformation and spiritual growth. The mentees discover new insights that enrich their spiritual life. Join the journey because

SHE inspires, SHE leads, SHE encourages, SHE gives, SHE serves, SHE worships, and SHE evolves.
